1. overflow: visible

Ye content bahar nikal jayega, default yahi hota hai. Koi scroll nahi aayega.

2. overflow: hidden

Ye content jo box ke bahar hai, kat jayega. Dikhega hi nahi, scroll bhi nahi.

3. overflow: scroll

Isme hamesha scrollbar dikhega, chahe content chhota ho ya bada. Force scroll.

4. overflow: auto

Best value! Jarurat padne par hi scrollbar aayega. Content bada hua to scroll, chhota to nahi.
